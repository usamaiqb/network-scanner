Network Scanner is a fast, lightweight, and privacy-focused tool for discovering and analyzing every device on your WiFi network. Whether you are a network administrator, a tech enthusiast, or simply curious about who is connected to your router, Network Scanner gives you instant visibility into your local area network (LAN) - completely offline, with no ads, tracking, or data collection.

<b>Key Features:</b>

• Fast WiFi network scanning to discover all devices on your local network
• Ping sweep with TCP fallback to detect devices that block ICMP
• Detailed device information including IP address, MAC address, and hostname
• MAC vendor lookup and detection of randomized (private) MAC addresses
• OS fingerprinting to identify Windows, Linux, macOS, routers, and printers
• Port scanner to identify open ports and running services
• Full port scan of all 65,535 ports for advanced network analysis
• mDNS / Bonjour discovery for AirPlay, Chromecast, printers, SSH, SMB, and more
• Device type detection and categorization
• Material Design 3 interface with a modern, intuitive UI
• Custom devices: rename devices and assign your own icons
• Interface selection (Wi-Fi, Ethernet, VPN) and customizable scan settings

<b>Who Is on My WiFi?</b>

• Find IP addresses of all devices connected to your WiFi network
• Discover unknown or unauthorized devices on your network
• Identify device types - smartphones, laptops, TVs, routers, printers, NAS, and more
• Check open ports to improve your network security
• Troubleshoot network connectivity issues
• Monitor your home or office network

<b>Privacy First:</b>

• No ads or tracking
• No analytics or data collection
• No external internet connection - all traffic stays on your local network
• All scanning happens locally on your device
• Minimal permissions requested
• 100% open source (GPL-3.0)

<b>Permissions Explained:</b>

Network Scanner only requests permissions essential for network scanning:
• Network access - To communicate with devices on your network
• WiFi state - To check if you're connected to WiFi
• Nearby WiFi devices - To find devices on your local network. On Android 16 and later, Android requires this permission for all local network communication, so scanning cannot work without it
• Location (optional, never used for tracking) - Only to read your WiFi network name (SSID). Scanning works without it

Network Scanner is completely ad-free, tracker-free, and respects your privacy. All code is open source and auditable.
